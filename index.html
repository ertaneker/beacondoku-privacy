English

Beacondoku ("the app") is an offline logic puzzle game. We do not require an account and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location or any other personal information ourselves.

  • Data stored on your device: game progress, stars, settings (sound, music, vibration, language) and daily puzzle status are stored locally on your device and never sent to us.
  • Advertising: the app shows ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Where required, a consent form is shown at first launch and can be reopened from Settings → Privacy options.
  • No sale of data: we do not sell or share personal data with anyone other than the ad provider described above.
  • Children: the app is not directed to children under 13.
  • Deleting data: uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.
  • Changes: we may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.

Deutsch
